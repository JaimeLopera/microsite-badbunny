Biography

Benito Antonio Martínez Ocasio, known worldwide as Bad Bunny, is a Puerto Rican rapper, singer and songwriter who took Latin trap and reggaeton from the island to the biggest stages on the planet.

Early life

Bad Bunny was born on March 10, 1994, in Bayamón and grew up in Vega Baja, Puerto Rico. As a child he sang in his church choir and listened to salsa, merengue and reggaeton. While studying audiovisual communication at the University of Puerto Rico at Arecibo, he worked part-time as a supermarket bagger and uploaded his first songs to SoundCloud.

Rise to fame

His online songs caught the attention of a record label, and in 2016 he signed his first deal. His debut album, X 100PRE, arrived in 2018. In 2020 he released YHLQMDLG and El Último Tour del Mundo, the latter becoming the first all-Spanish-language album to reach number one on the Billboard 200. In 2022, Un Verano Sin Ti became the first all-Spanish-language album nominated for Album of the Year at the Grammy Awards.

Global impact

In January 2025 he released Debí Tirar Más Fotos, a love letter to Puerto Rico that mixes salsa, bomba and reggaeton. In February 2026 it won Album of the Year at the Grammy Awards, the first time a Spanish-language album has taken the top prize. A week later, he headlined the Super Bowl LX halftime show on February 8, 2026. His stadium world tour began in November 2025.
